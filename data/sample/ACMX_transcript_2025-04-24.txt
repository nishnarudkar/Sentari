TITLE: Acme Industrial Q1 2025 Earnings Call (SYNTHETIC)
Operator: Good morning and welcome to the Acme Industrial first quarter 2025 earnings conference call. [Operator Instructions]
Maria Chen -- CEO: Thank you. We delivered a strong first quarter. Revenue grew 12% year over year, driven by robust demand across our automation and aerospace segments. Order backlog reached a record high, and customer demand remains strong. We are raising our full-year guidance and now expect revenue growth of 10% to 12%. Our management team is confident in the outlook.
David Osei -- CFO: Gross margin expanded 180 basis points to 34.2% on favorable mix and productivity gains. Operating margin improved as we lowered our cost base. We generated record free cash flow, and our liquidity position is strong with over 1.2 billion dollars of cash and an undrawn revolving credit facility. We repaid 200 million dollars of debt and our leverage ratio improved. We do not expect any material impact from ongoing litigation matters.
Operator: We will now begin the question-and-answer session. Our first question comes from Alan Reyes with Northern Securities.
Alan Reyes -- Analyst: Thanks. Can you talk about the second half? Is demand really as strong as the backlog suggests, and how sustainable is the margin expansion?
Maria Chen -- CEO: Well, we believe demand should remain healthy, although visibility beyond the third quarter is limited. Some customers have been pushing out orders, and we may see some softness in aerospace.
David Osei -- CFO: On margins, we expect input costs to be a headwind in the second half, and pricing may not fully offset that. We are cautious about the tariff situation, and it is difficult to predict the impact.
Alan Reyes -- Analyst: And the litigation you mentioned in the filings?
David Osei -- CFO: There is an ongoing patent dispute. We cannot comment on the details, but we could face a material charge if the outcome is unfavorable. We are monitoring it closely.
Operator: That concludes our question-and-answer session.
