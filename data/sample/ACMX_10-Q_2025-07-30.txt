TITLE: Acme Industrial 10-Q Q2 2025 - MD&A (SYNTHETIC)
Net sales increased 6% compared with the prior-year quarter, primarily due to higher volumes in automation, partially offset by lower aerospace demand. Gross margin decreased 90 basis points due to higher input costs and tariffs. We have been named as a defendant in a patent infringement lawsuit, and an adverse outcome could have a material adverse effect on our results of operations. As of June 30, we had 900 million dollars of cash and cash equivalents and 150 million dollars outstanding under our revolving credit facility. Our credit agreement contains financial covenants, and a failure to comply could restrict our access to liquidity.
