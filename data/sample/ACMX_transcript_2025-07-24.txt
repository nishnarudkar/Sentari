TITLE: Acme Industrial Q2 2025 Earnings Call (SYNTHETIC)
Operator: Good morning and welcome to the Acme Industrial second quarter 2025 earnings conference call.
Maria Chen -- CEO: Thank you. The second quarter was solid. Revenue increased 6% year over year, in line with our expectations. Demand in automation remained healthy, but aerospace orders declined as customers delayed deliveries. We are maintaining our full-year guidance and now expect revenue growth at the low end of the range.
David Osei -- CFO: Gross margin contracted 90 basis points to 33.3% as input cost inflation and tariffs weighed on profitability. Operating margin declined year over year. Free cash flow was lower on higher working capital. Our liquidity remains adequate with 900 million dollars of cash, and we drew 150 million dollars on our revolver. Litigation expenses increased this quarter as the patent dispute moved toward trial.
Operator: We will now begin the question-and-answer session. Our first question comes from Alan Reyes with Northern Securities.
Alan Reyes -- Analyst: You cut the guidance range. Should we expect further cuts? And why did you draw on the revolver?
Maria Chen -- CEO: We are not able to say. Demand conditions are uncertain, and we could see additional pressure in the second half if customers keep delaying projects.
David Osei -- CFO: We drew on the revolver as a precaution. Our covenant headroom is tighter than last year, and we are evaluating our options. We are unable to quantify the litigation exposure and it may be significant.
Operator: That concludes our question-and-answer session.
